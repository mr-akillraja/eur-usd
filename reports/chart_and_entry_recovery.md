# Chart startup and missed-entry fixes — 2026-09-30

The prior chart setup could fail during page loading. A separate execution bug could discard a qualifying one-minute entry when its matching thirty-second candle arrived late. Both are corrected without changing strategy thresholds.

## File-by-file changes

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| [bot/chart_indicators.py](../bot/chart_indicators.py) | Waits for actual chart controls to become visible (up to 15 seconds); configures the candle interval independently before opening indicators; retries configuration at most three times using the actual remaining UI state. Every retry removes extras and preserves matching instances, avoiding duplicate indicators after a partially successful action. Final success still requires exact timeframe and period readback. |
| [multi_agent_orchestrator.py](../multi_agent_orchestrator.py) | Removes new_entry as the submission eligibility flag. A logged 1m snapshot no longer consumes that entry while the matching 30s candle is absent. A newly completed matching snapshot may qualify inside the unchanged five-second window. Once an order attempt starts, the existing per-entry timestamp, execution lock and unresolved-order state still prevent another submission. Stale confirmations are rejected before payout work and checked again immediately before submission. |
| [tests/test_chart_display.py](../tests/test_chart_display.py) | Adds delayed-menu and partial-save recovery cases; verifies exact settings and no duplicates. |
| [tests/test_execution_state.py](../tests/test_execution_state.py) | Runs actual BUY and SELL passing candle fixtures through the real gate, supervisor, risk manager and journal, replacing only broker I/O. A 30s response arriving two seconds after the 1m close submits exactly once; one arriving after six seconds submits nothing. |

## Why the supplied run placed no trade

The prior run contained 10 unique 1m snapshots and no fully passing gate in either direction. No qualifying setup reached execution.

Most SELL candidates were rejected by the existing RSI band of 35–50, despite the downward trend. For example, 03:47 had RSI31.20 and 03:50–03:52 were also below35. At03:49 RSI was within band but rising and the confirmation failed the breakout requirements. BUY trend/EMA alignment was bearish. The chart failure did not change the native strategy candles.

The delayed-response bug is an additional missed-opportunity defect found during the audit; it is not claimed as the cause of the recorded RSI rejections. Forcing an order or widening RSI bands would change the requested strategy and was not done.
